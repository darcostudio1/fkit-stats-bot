# Privacy Policy for FK!T Stats Bot

**Effective Date:** May 2, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how the FK!T Stats Bot (“the Bot”) collects, uses, and protects your information.

## 1. Information Collected

- The Bot collects only the information you provide through slash commands (e.g., player stats, Discord user ID, timestamps).
- No sensitive personal data (such as email, address, or payment info) is collected.

## 2. How Information is Used

- Your data is used solely to display alliance stats and send reminders within your Discord server.
- Data is not shared with third parties.

## 3. Data Storage

- Data is stored securely using Supabase.
- Only authorized server administrators can access the stored data.

## 4. Data Retention

- Data is retained as long as your Discord server uses the Bot, or until requested to be deleted by a server admin.

## 5. User Rights

- You may request deletion of your data by contacting a server admin or the bot owner.

## 6. Changes to this Policy

- This policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the Bot after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

## 7. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the bot owner via Discord or GitHub.
